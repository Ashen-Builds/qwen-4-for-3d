# Fresh BF16 CCORE Babylon diagnostic

This is the fresh untouched CCORE BF16 control's **mechanically repaired diagnostic**, used for the inspected Thinking48/72 comparisons. It is separate from the historical native/Q8 reference. The raw source fails; this is not a raw benchmark pass.

[Original source](original.html), [assisted source](generated.html), [repair patch](repair.patch), [GLB](asset.glb), [validation](asset.glb.validation.json) and [provenance](provenance.json) preserve the evidence. The actual 2,040,220-byte GLB was exported and reopened with 13 clips/35 channels and zero validator errors/warnings.

The layout was more coherent than the Astra diagnostics, but still had six villagers rather than sixteen, no authored leg walking and disconnected palm fronds. Its water highlights/cloud drift survived export. No source-specific correction was used as training data.

The [Thinking48 comparison screenshots](../../checkpoints/astra-thinking48-diagnostic/README.md) use this exact control GLB. See the [final report](../../../docs/overnight-final-20261117.md). This package includes no weights or raw training corpus.
